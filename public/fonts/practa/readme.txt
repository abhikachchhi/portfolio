PRACTA is a free sans-serif font that embodies a futuristic aesthetic, perfect for both titles and paragraphs.

This font is the second typeface designed by Mauvais Jour, a creative studio based in France.

PRACTA supports the most commonly used Latin characters, including letters and numbers.

This font is free to use. All we ask is for credit and that you send us your creation so we can share it with our community.
